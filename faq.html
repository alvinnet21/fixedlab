FAQ

Good questions.

Quick answers about repairs, pricing and how we work.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the device and the problem. We check it first and give you a clear price — nothing happens until you say yes.

How long will it take?

Many jobs are done the same or next day. If a part needs ordering, we'll tell you how long up front.

Do I need to bring my device in?

We can pick up your device from you, or you can bring it to us in Mascot, NSW — whichever is easier.

Will I lose my photos and files?

We protect your data on every job. If there's any risk, we'll talk about a backup first. We never look through your personal files.

What if it can't be fixed?

We'll explain your options — including saving your data and helping you pick a replacement.
