FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Just ask.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the device and the fault. We'll diagnose it first and give you a clear, fixed quote — nothing goes ahead until you approve it.

How long will it take?

Many common jobs (software issues, virus removal, battery and screen swaps when parts are in stock) are done same or next day. If parts need ordering, we'll give you a realistic timeframe up front.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes. On-site visits are available across Sydney for setup, networking, printers and troubleshooting. Some hardware repairs are best done on the bench, and we'll let you know if that's the case.

Will I lose my files?

We take care to protect your data on every job. If there's any risk to your files, we'll talk to you about a backup before we start. Your data stays private — we never browse personal files.

What if it can't be fixed?

No fix, no fee. If a repair isn't possible or isn't worth the cost, we'll explain your options — including recovering your data and helping you choose a replacement.
